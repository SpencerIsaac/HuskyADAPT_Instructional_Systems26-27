# Safety Precautions
- Ensure that you are in a well-ventilated room. Turn on a fan if possible.
- Ensure soldering iron cords will not be tripped over.
- Keep your hands away from the iron tip, soldering connection, or anything that has recently touched the tip.

# Soldering Procedure

## Before Soldering

> ✏️ **Note**: For the purposes of this guide, we will be using the Light-Up Gear Car. Make sure you find the correct pins for your toy! Your motherboard and overall toy will likely be different than the images used in this manual.

Before starting the soldering procedures, you should already have the following complete:
- Tested toy with batteries
- Identified toy's power operated features (e.g. sounds, lights, motor)
- Disassembled toy to access circuit board.

## Step 1 - Identifying Pins for Soldering
> ✏️ **Note**:  If you are using a toy adaption manual, the appropriate pins should already be identified. We still recommend testing!

### 1. Choose a pin combination to check
> ⚠️ **Caution**: If the wires or circuitboard start to heat up, STOP! It is likely not the correct combinations of pins, and you don't want to burn yourself or damage the toy.

Touch each end of the test wires to different pins on the board. Try different pin combinations until the desired toy output/s turn on (ie. motor=movement, speaker=sound, lights=light)

For our example toy, we wanted the adapted switch to turn the car's lights on. Here is our final pin combination, connected with the test wire, toggling the lights on.
<img src="kalm/testing-pins.jpg" alt="Correct Light-up Car pins" />

### 2. Remove batteries from toy
> ⚠️ **Caution**: THIS IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT. DO NOT BEGIN SOLDERING YOUR TOY UNTIL BATTERIES HAVE BEEN REMOVED.

Remove batteries from the toy. Failure to remove batteries can lead to harm. Please stay safe!!!

## Step 2 - Check adaptor jack fit

Make sure the ends of the jack are long enough to reach the identified pins without strain.

If the wires are not long enough, use the Nippers to strip the outer wire.

## Step 3 - Setting up Soldering Station

<img src="kalm-sold/soldering-tools.jpg" alt="Process photo" />

1. Wear safety goggles

2. Turn on the fan
> ⚠️ **Caution**: Don't breathe in toxic fumes!!

3. Check sponge on the iron stand to make sure it is moist

4. Let the soldering iron sit in the coil stand until the temperature reaches ~800

Optional: Use the clippers on the magnifying glass to hold the first wire onto the correct pin.

## Step 4 - Begin Soldering by Melting a Drop of Metal Wire on the Pin

> ⚠️ **Caution**: Do NOT touch the tip to check if it is warm. Test on the sponge and watch for water vapor.

Pull some metal wire from the coil, and touch the tip of the iron to the wire. Hold for a few seconds until the wire begins to melt. The melted solder should collect into a bead at the tip of the iron.

<img src="" alt="Process photo" />

## Step 5 - Attach to wire and circuit board

Hold the bead of solder to the pin and wire on the circuit board. The bead should connect the wire to the pin.

If the solder does not connect correctly, use the iron to reheat the solder and adjust as needed. You can also melt more solder off of the solder spool.

> ⚠️ **Caution**: Make sure soldered pins do not touch each other

## Step 6 - Tug Test

Wait 5 seconds after removing the iron. Tug the wire gently to make sure it is properly attached to the circuit board.
